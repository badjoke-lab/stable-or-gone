## First tranche

### SBC
- Visa, 2026-06-04: SBC is a U.S.-dollar-backed stablecoin issued by Brale and natively supported on Canton.
- The reviewed Visa source describes a proof of concept for institutional settlement. It does not establish unrestricted public access, reserve allocation, holder claim terms, redemption minimums, or administrative controls.

Primary source: https://visa.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/visa-and-brale-explore-private-stablecoin-settlement

### Movement USDCx
- Circle, 2026-03-25: USDCx on Movement is available through Circle xReserve.
- Circle states it is deployed by Movement, fully backed 1:1 by USDC held in Circle xReserve.
- Circle publishes the Movement mainnet token address `0xba11833544a2f99eec743f41a228ca6ffa7f13c3b6b04681d5a79a8b75ff225e`.
- This is a protocol-issued/deployed USDC-backed stablecoin. Do not manufacture a conventional corporate legal-issuer claim.

Primary sources:
- https://www.circle.com/blog/usdcx-on-movement-now-available-via-circle-xreserve
- https://developers.circle.com/xreserve/references/supported-blockchains-and-domains

### USDU
- Universal Digital Intl Limited launched USDU on 2026-01-29.
- Universal is registered by the CBUAE as a Foreign Payment Token Issuer and regulated by the FSRA in ADGM with permission to issue a Fiat-Referenced Token to Professional Clients.
- USDU reserves are stated to be 1:1 U.S. dollars held in safeguarded onshore accounts at Emirates NBD and Mashreq.
- Universal states monthly independent reserve attestation.
- Professional Client scope and UAE use restrictions must remain explicit.

Primary source: https://www.universal.ae/news/universal-launches-uaes-first-central-bank-registered-usd-stablecoin

### USDKG
- The USDKG/Gold Dollar official site describes USDKG as a 1:1 USD-pegged stablecoin fully backed by physical gold.
- It states reserves are held in audited custody within Kyrgyzstan and supported by the Ministry of Finance of the Kyrgyz Republic.
- Direct minting and redemption are stated to be available to institutional clients subject to KYC/AML and issuer procedures; retail users access liquidity through supported exchanges.
- The exact authorized issuing legal entity is not established in the reviewed homepage/about material and must not be guessed.

Primary sources:
- https://www.usdkg.com/
- https://www.usdkg.com/about

### litUSD
- Stably states litUSD launched on Ethereum in August 2025.
- Brale is identified as issuer.
- Reserve backing is stated as cash and cash equivalents at 1:1.
- Verified Brale users can mint/redeem at parity through USDC or bank transfer, subject to Brale fees.
- Exact launch day and token contract identity require additional primary capture.

Primary source: https://www.stably.io/post/case-study-how-stably-helped-launch-litusd
