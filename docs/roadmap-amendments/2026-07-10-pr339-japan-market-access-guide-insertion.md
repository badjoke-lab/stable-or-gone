## Publication boundary

The article may discuss reviewed source-backed access observations. Raw monitoring findings, discovery leads, stale-state output, and unreviewed candidates remain private and unpublished.

The guide must state that:

- FSA register inclusion is not a government value guarantee or recommendation;
- Ripple's launch wording and the Japanese register/legal-route evidence are separate source layers;
- access is platform-, function-, network-, customer-, and date-specific;
- absence from the reviewed FSA handled-asset list does not by itself prove that another stablecoin is banned or illegal;
- future JPYSC external transfer must be recorded as a later change rather than backfilled into its initial account-internal launch state.
