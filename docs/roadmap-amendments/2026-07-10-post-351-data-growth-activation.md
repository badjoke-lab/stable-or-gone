## Dossier batch policy

PR #354, #355, and #357 deepen Tier A assets selected from the PR #353 baseline.

Each batch should normally cover no more than five assets.

Selection uses:

- significance;
- historical importance;
- comparison leverage;
- regulatory relevance;
- regional relevance;
- Market Access potential;
- evidence gap severity.

Dossier batches do not add new canonical assets unless explicitly approved as a combined growth scope.
