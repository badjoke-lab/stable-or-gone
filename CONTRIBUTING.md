# Contributing to Stable or Gone

Stable or Gone accepts source-backed corrections and registry improvements.

## Preferred routes

Use the contact page first:

https://sog.badjoke-lab.com/contact/

Use the Google Form for normal contact, non-public reports, missing records, broken links, and source suggestions.

Use GitHub Issues only for public, source-backed reports that can be discussed openly.

## What to include

For corrections, include:

- the affected Stable or Gone page URL
- the stablecoin, organization, event, or source involved
- what is wrong or missing
- what the record should say instead
- public source URLs supporting the change

Official sources, regulator notices, issuer pages, reserve reports, redemption terms, archived pages, and primary references are preferred.

## What not to submit

Do not submit:

- private keys
- seed phrases
- passwords
- wallet credentials
- exchange account details
- bank information
- identification documents
- sensitive personal information
- investment advice requests
- issuer, exchange, wallet, or redemption support requests
- token promotion or marketing copy

## Pull requests

Pull requests may be revised, delayed, or rejected. Records require evidence. Changes that weaken source quality, add unsourced claims, promote a token, or mix unrelated record-growth work into migration work may be declined.

## Review model

Stable or Gone is evidence-first. A report may be accepted, revised, left open for more evidence, or declined.
